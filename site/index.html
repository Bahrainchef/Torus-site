Good to know

Questions, answered.

What exactly is creatine?
A compound your body already makes and stores in muscle for short-burst energy. Supplementing tops those stores up. TORUS uses creatine monohydrate — the gold-standard, best-studied form.
Do I need a loading phase?
Nope. A steady 5g a day gets you there within a few weeks, no loading required — which is exactly how TORUS is built.
When should I take it?
Whenever you'll remember. Daily consistency beats timing, so hook it to something you already do — like your morning coffee.
Is it only for the gym?
No — the evidence covers strength and recovery, and research into focus and mental stamina is growing fast.
