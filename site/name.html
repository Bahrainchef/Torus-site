The name

A torus is a ring.

The shape you get when a circle is revolved around an axis outside itself — a closed loop, with no beginning and no end.

Three meanings, all true

One word, doing a lot of work.

◍

The shape

The ring around the wordmark isn't decoration and it isn't a container — it's a picture of what the word means. Rare in naming, and why the identity needs no tagline.

◍

The muscle

In classical Latin, torus means a swelling — specifically the swell of a muscle under tension. Ovid and Virgil used it that way. For a creatine brand, an unusually good accident.

◍

The loop

Creatine works through the phosphocreatine system, recycling ADP back into ATP — a closed loop running continuously in your cells. And it only works through daily repetition.

Never break the loop.

A torus is a closed loop — and creatine only works if you never break it.

The principle

Bold without shouting.

Saturation and restraint are separate choices. TORUS leans on full, flavour-true colour and one confident geometric mark — and lets that do the talking. No loud claims it can't back up, no noise for its own sake. Just a good product, made easy to keep up with.
