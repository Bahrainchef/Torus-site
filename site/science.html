How it works

A loop, running in your cells.

Creatine helps regenerate ATP — your cells' short-burst energy currency — by recycling spent ADP back into ATP through the phosphocreatine system. It's a closed loop, which is exactly where the name comes from. Keep your stores topped up with a daily 5g and the loop keeps turning.
